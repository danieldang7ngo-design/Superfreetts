<h2>⚙️ Settings &amp; Configuration</h2>
<p>Go to <strong>AnkiVN Menu → Super Free TTS Settings</strong>. There are 2 tabs:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Configuration:</strong> Toggle TTS services on/off with a single click — <strong>green is enabled, red is disabled</strong>. Supports <strong>Offline</strong> models (Piper, Kokoro...) for advanced users (performance depends on your CPU).</li>
<li><strong>Preferences:</strong> Choose your <strong>interface language</strong> (Vietnamese / English), manage <strong>Cache</strong>, and configure system preferences.</li>
</ul>

<h2>📖 Quick Start Guide</h2>

<h3>Method 1 — Save audio files to cards <em>(for offline studying on mobile)</em></h3>
<ol>
<li>Go to <strong>Browse</strong> and select the cards you want to add audio to.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Super Free TTS → Generate Audio Files</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose a voice under <strong>Voice Selection</strong> (some voices support adjusting <strong>Rate</strong> and <strong>Pitch</strong>).</li>
<li>Use <strong>Mapping Rules</strong> to automatically assign voices by Deck or Note Type — no need to re-select every time.</li>
<li>Click <strong>Start</strong>.</li>
</ol>

<p>
<img src="https://a.l3n.co/ctleIb.png" alt="Super Free TTS - Settings"><br>
<img src="https://c.l3n.co/ctlvur.png" alt="Super Free TTS - Compatibility">
</p>

<h3>Method 2 — Stream audio directly while reviewing <em>(Realtime)</em></h3>
<ol>
<li>Go to <strong>Browse → Select cards → Super Free TTS → Realtime Configuration</strong>.</li>
<li>Paste this line into your <strong>Card Template</strong>:<br>
<code>{{tts vi_VN voices=EdgeTTS: Field_Name}}</code></li>
<li>Flip the card — the audio plays immediately. <strong>Zero storage space used.</strong></li>
</ol>

<blockquote>
⚠️ <strong>Important Note:</strong> This method requires Super Free TTS installed on your desktop Anki. If you review cards on mobile or share decks with others, audio <strong>will not play</strong>. In that case, please use <strong>Method 1</strong> to save audio directly into the cards.
</blockquote>
